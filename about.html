About us

More than a venue directory.

TS Group Africa is a B-BBEE Level 1, women-owned company providing full turnkey service solutions for clients across Africa. For more than 12 years, we have supported clients with professional events, hospitality marketing, conferences, brand activations, product launches, corporate gifts, exhibitions, advertising campaigns and travel coordination.

We manage the entire event process — from concept development, themes, guest-list management and registration, through to production, reporting and close-out.
